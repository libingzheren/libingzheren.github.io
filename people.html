Dr. Bingzhe Li is currently an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Texas at Dallas. Prior to that, Dr. Li was an assistant professor at Oklahoma State university, and a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, advised by Prof. David Du. He has received his Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, under the supervision of Prof. David Lilja.

His research spans systems for AI, agentic AI, and storage systems, with particular interests in efficient AI/ML infrastructure, agentic memory and reasoning, and emerging memory and storage technologies such as DNA data storage. He has authored or co-authored more than 70 research papers in leading conferences and journals, including USENIX FAST, ASPLOS, HPCA, SIGMOD, NeurIPS, ICLR, ACL, DAC, ICDE, CVPR, and FPGA, as well as IEEE and ACM Transactions and journals such as IEEE TC, IEEE T-ED, ACM TECS, ACM TOS, ACM TOMPECS, and ACM JETC.

Dr. Li has served on conference organizing committees and technical program committees and as a reviewer for major conferences and journals in computer systems, storage, and computer architecture. His research has been recognized with an NSF CAREER Award in 2025, a Best Paper Nomination at ICCD 2021, and selection as the IEEE Transactions on Computers Featured Paper of the Month in March 2021.
